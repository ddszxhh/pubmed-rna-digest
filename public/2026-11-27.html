RNA Interference for Insect Pest Management: Linking dsRNA Structure-Function Relationships, Delivery Biology, and Field Performance.

RNA干扰用于害虫治理：连接dsRNA结构-功能关系、递送生物学与田间性能

Murtaza G, Qayyum N, Shoukat U, Munawar N, Qayyum MA, Tariq H, Chen YM, Hussein HS

酶/系统类型: 该论文为综述，不聚焦单一RNase III酶；涉及昆虫RNAi通路中的dsRNA切割核糖核酸酶（如Dicer，属RNase III家族）及外源dsRNA在昆虫体内的核酸酶降解过程，但未具体限定Class I RNase III、Drosha、Mini-III、Rnt1p或Pac1等类型。
机制要点: 外源dsRNA的活性取决于其分子结构、细胞外稳定性、与递送材料的相互作用、细胞摄取、胞内运输及进入RNAi机器的可及性；昆虫生理差异（如核酸酶活性、肠道屏障、内体加工和系统运输）导致RNAi敏感性不同。dsRNA被Dicer等RNase III类酶加工为siRNA后引导序列特异性基因沉默，而核酸酶降解和递送屏障限制其效力。
工程化与应用: 综述讨论了基于纳米材料的制剂（壳聚糖、脂质体、层状双氢氧化物）以及微生物、共生菌介导、寄主诱导基因沉默（HIGS）和喷雾诱导基因沉默（SIGS）等递送与表达平台；涉及制剂可重复性、生产成本、环境归趋、非靶标暴露和监管要求等应用考量，但未涉及具体蛋白突变设计或结构域融合等酶工程改造。
关键结果: 该综述未报告原始实验数据，而是系统整合了dsRNA结构-功能关系、递送生物学与田间性能之间的关联，指出RNAi应作为害虫综合治理的补充组分，田间应用需物种特异性优化和基于证据的环境评估。

RNA interference (RNAi) is a sequence-guided approach to insect pest management, but its efficacy depends on more than the choice of an essential target gene. The biological activity of exogenously applied double-stranded RNA (dsRNA) is shaped by its molecular properties, extracellular stability, interactions with delivery materials, cellular uptake, intracellular trafficking, and access to the RNAi machinery. This review examines these linked determinants and considers how differences in insect physiology contribute to variation in RNAi susceptibility. We discuss target-gene selection and sequence specificity alongside nuclease activity, gut barriers, endosomal processing, and systemic transport. Particular attention is given to nanomaterial-based formulations, including chitosan, liposomes, and layered double hydroxides, as well as microbial, symbiont-mediated, host-induced gene silencing (HIGS), and spray-induced gene silencing (SIGS) platforms. Their potential benefits are assessed in relation to formulation reproducibility, production costs, environmental fate, non-target exposure, and regulatory requirements. By connecting dsRNA structure-function relationships with delivery biology and application performance, this review identifies mechanistic questions and validation steps that must be addressed to develop reliable RNAi-based tools. RNAi is best regarded as a complementary component of integrated pest management, with field deployment requiring species-specific optimization and evidence-based environmental assessment.
