Natural herbal products suppress hepatocellular carcinoma by inhibiting microRNA-21-5p biogenesis.

天然草药产物通过抑制microRNA-21-5p生物发生抑制肝细胞癌

Pu W, Ouyang X, Luo G, Li J, Xu F, Sun D, Li M, Tan S

酶/系统类型: Class III RNase III 酶 DICER（负责将pre-miR-21加工为成熟miR-21-5p）
机制要点: 研究聚焦于pre-miR-21被DICER切割成熟为miR-21-5p的关键步骤。小分子天然产物liriodendrin和icariin通过结构导向虚拟筛选靶向pre-miR-21的腺嘌呤-29（A29）凹槽，直接结合该位点，破坏pre-miR-21与DICER的相互作用，从而选择性抑制miR-21-5p的成熟加工，而不广泛影响所测试的miRNA谱。该机制揭示了A29凹槽作为pre-miR-21上功能主导的位点特异性结合口袋，通过阻断底物识别/加工来调控致癌miRNA的生物发生。
工程化与应用: 涉及基于结构的虚拟筛选和小分子天然产物发现，定义了pre-miR-21结合的两亲性药效团（芳香核心加末端糖基）。研究实现了对pre-miR-21/DICER相互作用的位点特异性干扰和miRNA成熟的选择性抑制，属于RNA靶向小分子重编程策略；鉴定的先导结构可用于开发miRNA导向的肝细胞癌治疗药物，具有诊断/治疗开发生物技术应用价值。
关键结果: Liriodendrin和icariin直接结合pre-miR-21的A29凹槽，破坏pre-miR-21/DICER相互作用，并在所测试miRNA谱中优先抑制miR-21-5p成熟；两种化合物有效抑制HCC细胞增殖和异种移植模型中的肿瘤生长，验证了A29凹槽为功能主导的位点特异性结合口袋。

MicroRNA-21-5p (miR-21-5p) is a well-established oncogenic driver frequently overexpressed in hepatocellular carcinoma (HCC) due to dysregulated miRNA biogenesis. The processing of precursor microRNA-21 (pre-miR-21) by the RNase III enzyme DICER is a critical maturation step, yet achieving specific inhibition of pre-miR-21 remains a significant challenge. Herein, by using structure-based virtual screening targeting the adenine-29 (A29) groove of pre-miR-21, we identified two small-molecule natural products, liriodendrin (from Eucommia ulmoides Oliv.) and icariin (from Epimedium brevicornu Maxim), as potent and selective binders of pre-miR-21, defining an amphiphilic pharmacophore (an aromatic core plus terminal sugars) for pre-miR-21 binding. Mechanistic characterization revealed that both compounds directly engaged the A29 groove of pre-miR-21, thereby disrupting the pre-miR-21/DICER interaction and preferentially inhibiting miR-21-5p maturation in the tested miRNA profile. Functionally, these phytochemicals potently inhibited HCC cell proliferation and tumor growth in xenograft models. Collectively, liriodendrin and icariin are identified as novel natural-product inhibitors that target pre-miR-21 via the A29 groove, and their potential anticancer mechanism is partially disclosed. Our findings also validate the A29 groove as a functionally dominant, site-specific binding pocket, highlight natural herbal products as a rich source of RNA-targeting binders, and provide promising lead structures for the development of miRNA-directed HCC therapies.

Knyazev E, Antipenko I, Pirogov A, Chebotareva O, Tonevitsky A

机制要点: 缺氧条件下，滋养层细胞中ANG被强烈诱导，Dicer依赖性miRNA减少，tRNA切割重编程为产生32-33 nt的5' tRNA半分子（5'-halves）。这些Glu-tRF 5'半分子（如10_GluTTC_33和13_GluTTC_33）根据片段结构特征被选择性分选进入合体滋养层细胞外囊泡（STB-EVs），该分选过程与应激诱导的RNA结合蛋白YBX1变化相关。

Preeclampsia (PE) is characterized by placental ischaemia and a massive release of syncytiotrophoblast extracellular vesicles (STB-EVs). Transfer RNA-derived fragments (tRFs) form a substantial fraction of STB-EV RNA, but the mechanisms of their biogenesis and selective sorting under hypoxia remain unclear. To profile the tRF pool in placental tissue and STB-EVs in PE, alongside in vitro hypoxia in BeWo b30 cells, and to validate specific stress-induced tRFs. We re-analysed small RNA-seq from placental tissue and STB-EV fractions from normotensive and PE pregnancies, and from BeWo b30 treated with CoCl In vitro hypoxia reprogrammed small RNA processing in trophoblasts: Dicer-dependent microRNAs decreased, angiogenin (ANG) was strongly induced, and 32-33-nt 5' tRNA halves accumulated. In clinical samples, PE was associated with compartment-specific tRF changes: placental tissue showed modest remodelling, whereas STB-EVs displayed increased 5'-halves and altered tRF cleavage positions. Specific glutamate tRFs (Glu-tRFs) were consistently enriched in STB-EVs. RT-qPCR confirmed that chemical hypoxia induces 32-33-nt Glu-tRF 5'-halves in cells and promotes their selective loading into EVs. Rank-based analysis indicated that Glu-tRF sorting into STB-EVs depends on fragment structural features and is associated with stress-induced changes in the RNA-binding protein YBX1. Placental hypoxia triggers ANG-dependent biogenesis of 5' Glu-tRNA halves and their selective export in STB-EVs. The convergent fragments identified here, particularly 10_GluTTC_33 and 13_GluTTC_33, are promising non-invasive biomarkers of placental ischaemia and candidate paracrine effectors in PE.
