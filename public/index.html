Plant DICER-LIKE, dual measurement RNA rulers.

植物DICER-LIKE蛋白：双重测量RNA的分子标尺

Zhang Z, Wang C, Wang Q, Li S, Du J

酶/系统类型: 植物DICER-LIKE（DCL）蛋白，属于Class III RNase III家族的双链RNA切割核糖核酸酶；与动物Dicer同源，但种子植物DCL家族显著扩张，产生多种长度、结构和功能的sRNA。
机制要点: 植物DCL既作为产物RNA的分子标尺（测量切割产物的长度），又作为底物RNA的标尺（精确选择合适的目标底物），从而实现对底物选择性和产物特异性的双重调控；该机制支撑miRNA和siRNA两类主要sRNA的生物合成。
工程化与应用: 本文为综述，未涉及具体蛋白优化、突变设计、底物特异性重编程、结构域融合或可编程RNA切割等工程化改造；但所总结的底物选择性与产物特异性分子基础，可为植物DCL的工程化改造及生物技术应用提供理论依据。
关键结果: 综述总结了植物DCL底物选择性与产物特异性分子基础的最新进展，揭示植物DCL不仅像动物Dicer一样作为产物RNA的分子标尺，还作为底物RNA的标尺，从而能够精确选择合适的目标RNA。

Small RNA (sRNA)-mediated gene silencing plays critical roles in eukaryotic gene regulation. The biogenesis of the two major types of sRNA, microRNA (miRNA) and small interfering RNA (siRNA), relies on the type III endoribonuclease Dicer. Unlike animals, which typically harbor fewer Dicer proteins, seed plants possess a greatly expanded Dicer family of DICER-LIKE (DCL) proteins that produce multiple types of sRNAs with diverse lengths, structures, and functions. Accordingly, plant DCLs must both specifically select substrate RNAs and measure product RNAs with defined lengths. In this review, we summarize recent progress about the molecular basis underlying the substrate selectivity and product specificity of plant DCLs, revealing that these enzymes not only act as molecular rulers for product RNAs, as do their animal counterparts, but also serve as rulers for substrate RNAs, enabling precise selection of appropriate targets.
