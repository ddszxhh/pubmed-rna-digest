Apoplastic barriers and formulation requirements for RNAi-based control of tea fungal diseases.

质外体屏障与RNAi防控茶树真菌病害的制剂需求

Ahmed N, Junaid M, Kanwal M, Ullah R, Chen Z

酶/系统类型: 本文为综述，未聚焦单一RNase III酶；涉及真菌RNAi通路中的RNase III（如Dicer/Drosha同源物）及质外体RNase介导的dsRNA降解，并提及SID-1样转运蛋白和细胞外囊泡。
机制要点: dsRNA在植物质外体中易被RNase快速降解（本氏烟中半衰期2–6小时），其稳定性与转运依赖细胞外囊泡和SID-1样转运蛋白；真菌可通过上调RNase III等机制对抗RNAi。茶树病原菌（炭疽菌和饼病外担菌）的质外体RNase活性、EV丰度和SID-1同源物尚无数据。
工程化与应用: 综述提出纳米颗粒包封和RNase抑制剂等制剂策略保护dsRNA，并建议将递送化学与茶树质外体生物化学整合；未涉及蛋白突变设计或结构域融合，但强调可编程RNAi（HIGS/SIGS）在茶树病害防控中的应用潜力及候选靶标（几丁质合成酶、β-微管蛋白、CYP51、琥珀酸脱氢酶）的遗传冗余和预存抗性突变问题。
关键结果: 纳米包封靶向SdhC的dsRNA与SDHI杀菌剂协同作用，在葫芦白粉病14 dpi抑制率达69%；茶树病原菌中β-微管蛋白E198A等抗性突变和CHS基因5–7个同工型冗余构成靶标选择挑战；裸dsRNA无法在茶树质外体存活，需制剂干预。

Tea (Camellia sinensis) suffers severe yield losses (20-40%) from fungal diseases (anthracnose, blister blight, angray blight). Conventional fungicides face resistance and residue concerns. RNA interference (RNAi) strategies (HIGS/SIGS) offer sequence-specific control, but delivery barriers in the plant apoplast remain poorly characterized for tea. This review synthesizes mechanistic literature on dsRNA stability, trafficking, and uptake, using tea as a case study to identify knowledge gaps and formulation requirements. The plant apoplast presents critical bottlenecks: rapid RNase-mediated dsRNA degradation (half-life 2-6 h in Nicotiana benthamiana), dependence on extracellular vesicles, and SID-1-like transporters (confirmed in Fusarium). For tea pathogens (Colletotrichum and Exobasidium), no data exist on apoplastic RNase activity, EV abundance, or SID-1 orthologs. Candidate targets (chitin synthase, β-tubulin, CYP51, and succinate dehydrogenase) face genetic redundancy (5-7 CHS isoforms) and pre-existing resistance mutations (e.g., E198A in β-tubulin from tea isolates). Fungal counter-defenses include RNase III upregulation. Formulation advances (nanoparticle encapsulation, RNase inhibitors) protect dsRNA. Recent evidence shows nanoencapsulated dsRNA targeting SdhC synergizes with SDHI fungicides (69% suppression at 14 dpi) in cucurbit powdery mildew. Tea is a 'late beneficiary' of RNAi technology. Priority experiments are: (i) quantify tea apoplastic RNase activity; (ii) sequence RNAi pathway genes in tea pathogens; (iii) test encapsulated dsRNA on detached tea leaves. Without formulation interventions, naked dsRNA will not survive the tea apoplast. Progress requires integrating delivery chemistry with tea-specific apoplast biochemistry. © 2026 Society of Chemical Industry.
